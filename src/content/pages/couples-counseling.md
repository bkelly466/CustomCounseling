approachBody: |-
  I typically spend 8 to 12 weeks working with a couple. It is not an open-ended dialogue where we rehash the same issues over and over. The focus is to move you out of reactivity and into conscious responses to each other's needs.

  It works best when both partners are willing to fight for each other. Otherwise one person's growth can feel to the other like being left behind.
