Addiction is lonely. Whether it's alcohol, drugs, or porn, the cycle of promising yourself "this is the last time" wears you down. You don't have to untangle it alone.

Individual therapy is one of the most effective approaches to addiction. It's a **confidential space for you**, with no lectures and no shame.
