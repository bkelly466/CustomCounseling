You have given everything to this country: your body, your mind, your family, and your time. **I'm glad you're here.**

I have worked with many veterans, so I understand how hard it is to even consider therapy. I know the loops you go through to find a counselor who fits your coverage, and then to find one you actually trust.

Whatever you have going on, I would love to help you during this time.
