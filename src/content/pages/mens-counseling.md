As **men**, we tend to bottle up our emotions. Maybe you were taught young to keep it to yourself. Maybe it feels like nobody really wants to hear it.

So it builds. The stress from work comes home. The short fuse gets shorter. And the thoughts you have nowhere to unload keep circling at 2 a.m.

Finding a therapist who actually gets how men think, and what we need, is hard. **I get it, and I'm here to help.**
