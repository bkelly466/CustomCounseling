  - After our free 15-minute phone call, I email you a couple of forms to sign. No portals, no passwords.

  - A phone or computer
  - A private spot where you can talk freely
  - Headphones if you want extra privacy
  - That’s it
couplesBody: Most couples join together on one screen at home. Separate devices work too, even if one of you is traveling. We'll figure out what works best for you both.
privacyBody: Yes. Sessions happen over a secure Google Meet link, and what we talk about stays between us, the same as it would in an office.
techFailureBody: If the call drops, I'll send a fresh link right away. If we can't reconnect, we finish by phone or reschedule the remaining time at no charge.

**Online counseling can be just as effective as in-person.** You can meet me wherever you are. Social anxiety, time constraints, crippling depression, whatever makes leaving the house hard, none of it has to stand between you and getting help.

Some of my clients open up more from their own couch than they ever did in an office. Your space is familiar. That helps.
